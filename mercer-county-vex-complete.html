🎯 Why VEX IQ Robotics Matters

Robotics isn't just about building machines—it's about building futures

🧠

Problem-Solving Skills

100% Engagement

Students learn critical thinking and engineering design through hands-on challenges that require creative solutions to real-world problems.

🤝

Teamwork

2x Communication Skills

Students work in teams, learning collaboration and communication skills essential for any career while building lasting friendships.

💻

Programming Experience

Real Coding

Learn block-based coding and Python while controlling actual robots. Students gain hands-on programming experience that translates to real-world applications.

🎓

College Success

Higher Education

Alumni have gone to WVU, Columbia, and other top universities with scholarships. Robotics experience gives students a competitive edge in college admissions.

💼

STEM Career Pathways

Career Ready

Opens doors to engineering, programming, and technology careers. Students develop skills that employers actively seek in the modern workforce.

🌟

Confidence & Life Skills

Personal Growth

Builds confidence, resilience, and the ability to learn from failure. Students develop grit and perseverance through competition and iteration.

🚀 Your Robotics Journey

From first-time builder to world champion competitor

1

Join Your School Team

Sign up at any of our 15 participating schools. No experience needed - just curiosity and enthusiasm!

2

Learn & Build

Work with coaches and teammates to design, build, and program your robot. Learn block coding or Python.

3

County Competition

Compete against 50+ local teams at the Mercer County Championships. Top teams advance to state.

4

State Championship

Represent Mercer County at the WV State Championships in Fairmont. Win awards and qualify for Worlds.

5

World Championship

Travel to Dallas, Texas to compete against teams from 55+ countries at the VEX Robotics World Championship!
