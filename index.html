Why Robotics Matters

Building skills that last a lifetime

💡 Critical Thinking

Students learn to analyze problems, test solutions, and iterate designs through hands-on engineering challenges that mirror real-world scenarios.

🤝 Teamwork & Leadership

Every team member plays a vital role, teaching collaboration, communication, and how to lead effectively in diverse groups.

🎯 STEM Excellence

Practical application of science, technology, engineering, and mathematics concepts in exciting, competitive environments that make learning fun.

💪 Resilience & Perseverance

Learning to overcome setbacks, debug problems, and keep pushing forward—essential skills for any career path and life in general.

🚀 Career Preparation

Direct exposure to engineering, programming, and project management gives students a competitive edge for college and careers.

🌟 Confidence Building

Achieving goals, competing successfully, and mastering complex skills builds self-confidence that extends to all areas of life.

Perfect For All Students

👶

Elementary (Grades K-5)

VEX IQ introduces young learners to robotics through age-appropriate challenges that develop problem-solving and creativity.

🎓

Middle School (Grades 6-8)

Students advance to more complex engineering challenges, deeper programming, and competitive strategy development.

🏅

High School (Grades 9-12)

VEX V5 provides college-level engineering experiences with advanced robotics, autonomous programming, and leadership opportunities.

Your Journey to World Championship

From beginner to world competitor in five steps

1

Join the Program

Register in August for the upcoming season. No experience needed—just bring curiosity and enthusiasm to learn!

2

Learn & Build

September through November: Learn robot design, construction, programming, and strategic thinking through hands-on workshops.

3

Compete Locally

Test your skills at regional tournaments, earning rankings and qualifying points while gaining valuable competition experience.

4

Advance to States

Top-performing teams advance to state championships, competing against West Virginia's best for a chance at Worlds.

5

World Championship

Excellence at states earns qualification to VEX Worlds—compete on the international stage with the best teams globally!
